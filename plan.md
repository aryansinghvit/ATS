This is the comprehensive architectural and product blueprint for the AI-Native Applicant Tracking System (ATS) SaaS.

## I. Product Definition & Core Philosophy

**What We Are Building:**
We are building a multi-tenant, AI-native Applicant Tracking System (ATS) designed as a B2B SaaS platform. It replaces legacy, manual-entry HR software (like Workday or Greenhouse) with a system that acts as a "Virtual Recruiter."

**The Core Philosophy: "Deterministic AI for Triage, Humans for Decisions"**
Startups and enterprises do not want black-box AI making autonomous hiring decisions, nor do they want a dumb Kanban board that requires recruiters to read 500 PDFs. This platform automates the tedious top-of-funnel work—ingestion, parsing, technical cross-referencing, and initial screening—so human recruiters only spend their time interviewing the top 10% of mathematically matched candidates.

**The System Boundaries:**
This ATS is the "Cloud Frontend & CRM Database." It operates completely independently from the Apple M3 MLX Resume Extraction Microservice you just built. The ATS captures candidate files from the web, securely routes them to your extraction microservice via webhook, and consumes the returning JSON to populate the user interface.

## II. Exhaustive Feature Specification by Module

### Module 1: Multi-Tenant Workspace & Access Control (RBAC)

To sell this as a SaaS, the platform must securely host multiple companies on the same database without data leakage.

* **Workspace Provisioning:** Automated generation of a secure tenant environment (`tenant_id`) the moment a founder subscribes. Each workspace gets a custom subdomain (e.g., `careers.startupname.com`), branded color schemes, and logo integration.
* **Database Isolation:** Row-Level Security (RLS) enforced at the PostgreSQL layer. A query executed by a recruiter at Company A physically cannot return candidate records belonging to Company B.
* **Strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):**
* **Admin / Founder:** Full billing access, API key management, pipeline stage editing, and EEO compliance data access.
* **Recruiter:** Can create jobs, trigger AI pre-screens, email candidates, and move cards across the Kanban board.
* **Hiring Manager:** Can view candidate scorecards, read resumes, and approve/reject candidates for their specific department.
* **Interviewer:** Restricted to a "blind view" of the candidate and a blank scorecard to submit their interview feedback, preventing interviewer bias.



### Module 2: Generative Job Requisitions

Founders and hiring managers hate writing job descriptions. This module turns a 45-minute chore into a 30-second workflow.

* **AI Job Description Generator:** A single text box where the user types a raw thought: *"I need a senior frontend dev who knows React, tailwind, and can help us migrate from AWS to GCP in the next 6 months."* The built-in LLM instantly generates a polished, inclusive, and legally compliant job description.
* **Automated Taxonomy Extraction:** Before the job goes live, the system parses the finalized job description and locks in the **Required Skills** and **Preferred Skills**. This data is sent directly to your extraction microservice to fuel the deterministic job-match scoring engine (built in Phase 4).
* **External Syndication:** One-click distribution of the finalized job post to LinkedIn, Indeed, and Google Jobs via API integrations.

### Module 3: Zero-Friction Career Portal & Ingestion

The candidate experience must be flawless to prevent high-quality talent from abandoning the application.

* **No-Login Application Dropzone:** Candidates do not create accounts. They simply drop their PDF/DOCX file into a secure web component.
* **The Ingestion Gateway (Connecting to your M3):** When a file is dropped, the ATS uploads it to a secure, temporary cloud bucket and fires a payload to your `POST /api/v1/resumes/extract` microservice endpoint.
* **Auto-Populated Fields:** The ATS waits for the JSON response from your microservice and instantly displays the parsed data to the candidate (Name, Email, GitHub, Skills) for a 5-second human verification before final submission.

### Module 4: Deterministic Triage & Interactive Pipeline

This is the core dashboard where recruiters spend 90% of their day.

* **The Match-Scored Kanban Board:** Candidates appear in the "Applied" column as visual cards. Instead of chronological sorting, cards are automatically ranked by the **Deterministic Match Score** (e.g., 85/100) generated by your extraction engine.
* **The Candidate Profile Modal:** Clicking a card opens a split-screen view.
* *Left Side:* The structured JSON data cleanly rendered (Skills tags, chronologically sorted Work Experience, Education, Projects).
* *Right Side:* The original PDF embedded in an iframe for manual verification.


* **Match Breakdown Badge:** A visual UI component detailing exactly *why* the candidate scored what they did (e.g., "✅ Matched: Python, FastAPI, Docker. ❌ Missing: Kubernetes").
* **Customizable Workflow Stages:** Drag-and-drop customization of hiring stages (Applied ➔ Tech Screen ➔ Take-Home ➔ Culture Fit ➔ Offer).

### Module 5: Semantic Talent Pool (Vector RAG)

Traditional ATS databases are "black holes" where past applicants are forgotten because boolean keyword searches fail.

* **Vector Embedding Pipeline:** Every time your microservice returns candidate JSON, the ATS converts their skills and project descriptions into vector embeddings and stores them in a vector database (like pgvector or ChromaDB).
* **Conversational Sourcing:** When a company opens a new job, the recruiter can search their *existing* database of past applicants using natural language: *"Find me an engineer who has built projects with Spring Boot."*
* **Automated Resurfacing:** The system proactively alerts recruiters: *"You just opened a Senior DevOps role. You have 14 past applicants in your database who are a 90%+ match for this new role."*

### Module 6: Automated Pre-Screening & Auditing

This is the ultimate high-ROI feature that justifies the monthly SaaS subscription.

* **Trigger-Based Asynchronous Interviews:** When a recruiter drags a candidate into the "Screening" column, the ATS automatically emails the candidate a secure link to a 5-minute automated screening portal.
* **Dynamic AI Question Generation:** The ATS looks at the candidate's missing skills from the match score and generates targeted questions (e.g., *"I see you have heavy React experience but lack the required Vue.js background. How would you approach transitioning to Vue?"*).
* **Scorecard Attachment:** The candidate submits their responses (text or audio). The system transcribes the audio, summarizes the response, flags confidence markers, and attaches a screening scorecard directly to the candidate's Kanban card.

### Module 7: Interview Coordination & Communication

Replacing the need for separate email threads and scheduling apps like Calendly.

* **Two-Way Calendar Sync:** OAuth integration with Google Workspace and Microsoft 365. The ATS reads the hiring manager's calendar and generates unique scheduling links showing only available free blocks.
* **Automated Email Workflows:** Trigger-based templates. Moving a card to "Rejected" queues a polite, delayed rejection email (to prevent the candidate from knowing they were rejected in 30 seconds). Moving a card to "Offer" triggers a congratulatory email with an integrated DocuSign/HelloSign payload.
* **Team Collaboration Feed:** An internal comment thread on every candidate card where interviewers `@tag` each other and leave structured 1-to-5 star ratings on technical and behavioral competencies.
